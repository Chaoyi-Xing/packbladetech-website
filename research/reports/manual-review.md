# Manual review before publication or quotation

As of 2026-10-09, the database contains 100 sourced candidates and 24 local `noindex` draft pages. None is an independently verified PackBlade Tech specification, stock claim or machine-fit guarantee.

1. **Application classification:** All 100 records were audited. The 66 serrated-category records are primarily source-identified by edge geometry; their individual sources do not establish a more specific cutting station. No category was moved to balance counts. The 14 film-category candidates are provisional research classifications because film substrate is not item-level documented. None is on the draft shortlist.
2. **Reference types:** Seven Stiles rows explicitly say “Replaces [brand] #[reference].” This is a documented supplier replacement claim, not an independently verified genuine OEM part number. The other 93 rows have an equipment brand but no confirmed OEM part number. Cadence SBIP identifiers are Cadence SKUs. PackBlade Tech IDs are internal IDs.
3. **Gloucester page 11:** Four item rows state “No slots”; adjacent grouped illustrations depict elongated slots. The row facts remain in each record. Illustration dimensions and slot drawings live only in `source-observations.json`, with no item assignment. `specification-discrepancies.csv` lists four unresolved illustration associations, not four proven contradictory SKU specifications. Obtain an item drawing or sample before adding any Gloucester reference to the publication shortlist.
4. **Tidland page 19:** ACE calls the items slitters but does not identify film as the substrate. OD/ID dimensions appear only in shared Class II/III illustrations and are stored as page observations. All 12 Tidland candidates remain in the research database; none is selected for a draft page until film application and item dimensions are confirmed.
5. **Other film-category candidates:** Two ACE Cryovac piercing blades likewise lack a documented film substrate. They remain research candidates only.
6. **Possible overlaps:** See `duplicate-review.md`. Bosch and Hayssen equipment references occur in both ACE and Stiles, while coatings or source precision differ. Cryovac PV01S004 and PV01S0004 are different printed strings. Do not merge by reference resemblance alone.
7. **Source access:** ACE individual product pages returned Cloudflare 403. The supplied official PDF is the accessible ACE evidence. Product-page top summaries and technical tables have not been compared, so absence of other conflicts is not established.
8. **Missing data:** No item-specific sachet replacement model was documented in the existing 100. Material is missing for 17 records; hardness and bevel angle are missing for all 100. Mounting-hole details are nearly absent. See `coverage.json` for all field counts.
9. **Images:** Supplier image URLs are research references only. No competitor image is in `source/images/` or the built site.

Before approving a draft, compare the source reference, dimensions, cutting edge, mounting features, material and machine context with the buyer's drawing, installed blade or equipment documentation. Keep review pages `noindex` and out of the sitemap until approved.
