# Serrated Packaging Knives

This category groups packaging knife references identified chiefly by their cutting-edge form: toothed, scalloped, zigzag or perforating. An equipment brand or tooth count alone does not establish the blade's cutting station or machine fit.

## Machinery applications

Packaging equipment may use these profiles for cutoff or perforation. Where a source confirms a specific bag-making or flow-wrapper position, the knife belongs in that application category even if its edge is serrated.

## Knife types

Supplier references include straight-body toothed, scalloped, zigzag and perforated profiles. T-shaped and double-sided toothed blades are also handled by enquiry. Check tooth form, mounting holes or slots, coating and material as separate part details.
