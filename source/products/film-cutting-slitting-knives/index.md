# Film Cutting & Slitting Knives

Film knives slit a moving web lengthwise, trim its edge or cut it across the web. These operations use different knife geometries, and the material being cut must be confirmed before a slitter reference is described as a film knife.

## Machinery applications

Film converting, rollstock packaging, and film-feed sections of packaging machines. Identify the cutting station before comparing replacement knives.

## Knife types

Circular and straight slitters, cross-cut knives and perforation blades are relevant types. For circular knives, record outer diameter, bore and thickness; for straight knives, record length, width, thickness and mounting details. Identify the film or web material when sending an enquiry.
