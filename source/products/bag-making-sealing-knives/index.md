# Bag Making & Sealing Knives

Bag-making equipment uses cutoff knives at or near the sealing jaws to separate finished bags. This category includes source-listed bagger blades and supplier-stated replacement references for vertical form-fill-seal equipment.

## Machinery applications

Vertical form-fill-seal baggers and related pouch equipment. A machine manufacturer's name alone does not establish the correct blade: jaw width, mounting features, and part revision also matter.

## Knife types

Documented examples include straight-body bagger cutting blades with source-listed length, width or thickness. A supplier's “replaces” reference is shown separately from its own SKU; it is not treated as an independently verified OEM part number. Edge details are shown only where the source provides them.
