# Sachet Cutting Knives

Sachet cutting concerns the transverse separation or controlled perforation of small packets formed from a packaging web. PackBlade Tech accepts enquiries for seasoning-sachet and similar packet-cutting knives using the existing blade, drawing or sample as the reference.

## Machinery applications

Single-lane and multi-lane sachet packaging may require individual packets, connected strips or a tear-off line. Specify the cut position relative to the seal and whether the web must be fully separated or left connected by perforations.

## Knife types

Common terms include transverse cutoff knife, perforating knife and tear-notch profile. The required edge form, mounting geometry and dimensions depend on the installed machine; provide a blade sample or drawing for a model-specific enquiry.
