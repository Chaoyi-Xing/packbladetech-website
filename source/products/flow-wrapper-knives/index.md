# Flow Wrapper Knives

Flow-wrapper knives cut at a defined station in horizontal form-fill-seal equipment. End-seal cutoff knives separate packs across the web; the required edge may be straight, toothed or zigzag according to the installed jaw assembly.

## Machinery applications

Horizontal form-fill-seal and flow-wrap machines use cross-cut or cutoff knives at the end-seal area. Confirm the machine model, jaw assembly, and existing blade reference before choosing a replacement.

## Knife types

Source-listed references include straight and zigzag flow-wrapper knives and a PFM cutoff-blade listing. A longitudinal slitter used elsewhere on the web path belongs in [Film Cutting & Slitting Knives](/products/film-cutting-slitting-knives/) when its substrate is documented.
