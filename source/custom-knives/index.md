# Custom Knife Solutions

<p class="page-intro">A drawing or physical sample is the most useful starting point for a custom knife enquiry.</p>

## What to prepare

1. A drawing with dimensions, or clear photographs of the existing blade and a sample reference.
2. A short description of the machine's cutting task and the material being cut.
3. The quantity required and any known part reference.

## Quotation workflow

Email the available information to [sales@packbladetech.com](mailto:sales@packbladetech.com). The part requirements can then be reviewed, missing details clarified, and a quotation discussed. A sample or drawing does not by itself confirm a final specification or machine fit.
