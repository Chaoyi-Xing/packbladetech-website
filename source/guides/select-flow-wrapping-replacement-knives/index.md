# How to Select Replacement Knives for Flow Wrapping Machines

Flow wrappers can have more than one cutting position. The knife at the end-seal jaws separates packs across the film web, while a slitter at another station cuts along the web. Start by identifying which position needs a replacement.

## Confirm the machine and jaw assembly

Record the equipment manufacturer, machine model and any jaw or knife-holder reference from the parts manual. Suppliers document smooth, toothed, zigzag, scissor-cut and cross-cut configurations for flow wrapping. The same machine family may use different knife arrangements, so an equipment model should be checked alongside the installed blade.

## Record the existing knife

Measure overall length, width and thickness. Sketch the hole or slot positions from a defined datum and note which face carries the cutting bevel. Photograph both faces and a close view of the edge. If the edge is toothed, record the tooth pattern; if it is straight, record that explicitly. A [PFM flow-wrapper cutoff blade reference](/products/flow-wrapper-knives/pfm-sp71cs0525-dd-flow-wrapper-cutoff-blade-pbt-fw-001/) provides published overall dimensions, but its machine model and material are not stated in the source, so those details must still be checked.

The cutting station matters when choosing a category. End-seal cutoff knives belong with [Flow Wrapper Knives](/products/flow-wrapper-knives/). A blade used for longitudinal film slitting belongs with [Film Cutting & Slitting Knives](/products/film-cutting-slitting-knives/).

## Check source references carefully

Separate the equipment model, any documented equipment part number, a supplier's replacement SKU, and PackBlade Tech's internal reference. Supplier descriptions can help locate a candidate, but they do not independently verify a different supplier's dimensions or compatibility. Compare the source record with the removed blade, drawing or machine documentation before requesting a quotation.

## Prepare the quote request

Email the machine model, cutting position, part references, dimensioned drawing or blade photos, the film or packaging material, and the quantity needed. State any unresolved details. PackBlade Tech can then clarify the part requirements; final specifications and fit must be confirmed before quotation.
