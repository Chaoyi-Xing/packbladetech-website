# How to Identify and Measure Packaging Machine Knives

A replacement enquiry starts with the installed blade and its location in the machine. A machine brand alone rarely identifies the exact knife: different jaw widths, mounting patterns and part revisions can use different blades. Record what you can confirm, and leave uncertain fields blank.

## Identify the cutting position

Note whether the blade cuts across a completed bag, cuts at the end-seal jaws of a flow wrapper, perforates a sachet web, or slits film along the web direction. That position helps distinguish a [bag-making cutoff knife](/products/bag-making-sealing-knives/) from a [flow-wrapper knife](/products/flow-wrapper-knives/) or [film slitter](/products/film-cutting-slitting-knives/).

Photograph the blade before removal if it is safe to do so. Capture both faces, the cutting edge, mounting holes or slots, and any marking. Keep the machine model and part number from the parts manual separate from a supplier's catalog SKU. For example, the [Hayssen 10137A0430 bagger knife reference](/products/bag-making-sealing-knives/hayssen-10137a0430-bagger-knife-pbt-bs-004/) records an equipment reference and a separate ACE item number.

## Measure a straight knife

Measure overall length, maximum width and body thickness with a suitable caliper. Record the original unit; 1 inch equals exactly 25.4 millimeters. Do not infer thickness from a photograph or convert a rounded catalog size into a tighter tolerance.

For every mounting feature, record its shape, size and position from a defined end or centerline. A sketch with hole-center distances, slot lengths and the edge orientation is often more useful than a single overall size. If the blade has teeth, record the tooth count or pitch, tooth form and whether the pattern runs along one or both edges. The [Multivac zigzag knife reference](/products/serrated-packaging-knives/multivac-79-912-7128-00-zigzag-toothed-knife-pbt-sp-003/) shows why edge profile should be recorded alongside body dimensions.

## Measure a circular knife

Record outer diameter, inner diameter or bore, thickness, and any keyway or mounting holes. Note whether the cutting bevel is on one face or both faces. For [film cutting and slitting knives](/products/film-cutting-slitting-knives/), record the substrate as well as the cutting position; a slitter designation alone does not establish a film application.

## Send a useful enquiry

Include the machine brand and model, existing part references, a dimensioned drawing, and clear blade photographs. Describe the material being cut and the desired cut, such as full separation or perforation. State the quantity required. If material grade, hardness or coating is unknown, say so rather than estimating.
