# Serrated vs Straight Packaging Knives: Applications and Differences

The cutting edge is one of the most visible differences between packaging knives, but an edge label alone does not specify a replacement. The blade still needs the correct body dimensions, mounting pattern, orientation and cutting position.

## Straight edges

A straight edge forms a continuous cutting line. Suppliers list straight cutoff knives for bag-making and flow-wrapper systems, and straight knives can also be used for transverse film cutting. The required bevel and mounting geometry depend on the machine and the opposing surface or blade. A straight edge should not be substituted for a toothed edge solely because the body dimensions appear similar.

## Serrated, scalloped and toothed edges

These edges use repeated peaks, curves or teeth along the cutting line. Supplier catalogs distinguish scalloped blades, zigzag profiles and tooth-count variants. For example, the [Hayssen scalloped-edge reference](/products/serrated-packaging-knives/hayssen-sbip62-scalloped-edge-packaging-knife-pbt-sp-015/) and [Multivac zigzag knife reference](/products/serrated-packaging-knives/multivac-79-912-7128-00-zigzag-toothed-knife-pbt-sp-003/) document different patterns. A tooth count or named pattern is useful only when combined with blade dimensions and mounting details.

Some blades use a perforating or pre-cut pattern so packs remain connected until separated later. Others make a full cutoff. Confirm which result the machine requires, especially for [sachet cutting](/products/sachet-cutting-knives/) and [bag-making](/products/bag-making-sealing-knives/) applications.

## What to compare before ordering

Record the installed blade's length, width and thickness; hole or slot geometry; cutting-edge direction; tooth profile or pitch; and any documented material or coating. Check the machine model, jaw or holder assembly, and part reference independently. A supplier's SKU is a catalog identifier, not proof of an OEM part number or fit.

Photographs can show edge style and mounting details, but a dimensioned drawing or sample is needed when the profile is unusual or worn. See [how to measure a packaging knife](/guides/identify-measure-packaging-machine-knives/) for a practical checklist.
